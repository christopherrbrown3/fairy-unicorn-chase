# Character art upgrade

Scope: Glender, Mia, and the shared unicorn cast; Experience mode. This extends
the existing playable storybook and does not redesign the interface or worlds.

Keep Mia blonde, in blue, riding Glender with fairy wings. Keep Glender's golden
mane, tail, and body stars. Every unicorn stays white with its existing mane
palette. Preserve forgiving play, controls, saves, and all eighteen worlds.

Direction: sculpted animated storybook characters with connected equine anatomy,
expressive layered eyes, soft facial color, swept tapered hair, silk clothing,
leather tack, small gold inlays, floral jewelry, and translucent veined wings.
Close-up detail must remain readable at the actual chase camera distance.

Motion: independent skeletons, four-contact gallop, bending knees, shared rider
suspension, a raised water-magic arm, blinking, and secondary hair/wing motion.
Respect pause and gentle motion. Share geometry and release instance skeletons.

Review: actual game screenshots at 1024x768 and 390x844, cast and close-up views
from the local character studio. This is a code-led character refinement; no
generated design comp or whole-site direction selection applies. The studio is
a local review tool and is not included in the production build.

Open boundary: owner approval of the new likenesses; physical iPad performance.
The work is a step toward richer stylized art, not a claim of AAA production parity.
