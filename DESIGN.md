# Visual direction

Mode: Experience. The real animated 3D world leads; menus recede around it.

Pinned brief: rounded storybook figures, magical pastel scenery, delightful and readable at a six-year-old's scale. Broad tracks carry the racing rhythm. Every theme has recognizable physical scenery rather than only a palette swap.

The title shows Mia riding Glender in the actual meadow. A large playful title on the left leaves the characters visible on the right. A blue Play button is the dominant action. World and collection menus form an illustrated adventure book with ten tappable entries.

Typography: self-hosted Baloo 2 (SIL Open Font License), rounded and sturdy. Body text uses a local sans-serif fallback. Ink #243e62, paper #fff8ef, blue #2874b8, gold #ffdc79. Soft tinted shadows and tactile rounded controls; no developer UI during play.

HUD: compact world label and unicorn distance at the top. Large translucent steering buttons in the bottom corners; water sits beside right steering. 100px+ primary controls. Pause remains top-right and safe-area spacing applies throughout.

Motion: pony gallop, breathing fairy wings, hovering potions, particles, camera damping and a group celebration. Reduced-motion preference limits camera and celebratory motion. No flashes.
