# Fairy Unicorn Chase
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated explicitly in the owner's brief: Three.js, JavaScript, Vite, static GitHub Pages, browser localStorage and Web Audio. No backend or paid service.

## Users
A six-year-old playing on an iPad in landscape, plus family playing on desktop. Small hands, little reading, forgiving controls.

## Product Purpose
Mia rides Glender in a friendly game of chase. Catch one white unicorn in each magical world; catch every friend to celebrate together.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Auto-run, left/right hold-to-steer, optional cosmetic water spray, sparkly potion speed boosts, automatic jumps, no lives or punitive failure. Saved collection and level unlocks. Public repository and custom domain fuc.christopherbrown.ai. Preserve all existing unrelated local work and DNS wildcard.

New game is available from the home and pause menus. Confirming resets collected unicorns and world unlocks, saves the cleared progress and begins a fresh chase in Rainbow Meadow. Sound, picture quality and gentle motion preferences stay the same. Cancel or Escape leaves progress untouched and returns to the same menu; a paused chase remains paused.

## Brand Commitments
Mia: blonde hair, blue dress, fairy wings and water powers. Glender: yellow mane and tail, stars on her body. Unicorns: white with distinct mane colors. Original storybook artwork and sounds; custom procedural fan-themed additions with no downloaded game assets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large icon-led touch targets, multitouch, keyboard equivalents, muted play, pause, reduced motion and orientation hints. Real device performance remains subject to owner testing.

The New game confirmation explains the reset before it happens, initially focuses Cancel, keeps keyboard focus within the dialog and restores focus to its menu button on cancellation.

## Installation, Offline Play & Releases

The game can be installed to a mobile Home Screen as a standalone PWA. The title screen offers browser installation or device instructions. A complete cached build supports offline play after its first successful online download. Custom unicorn icons and a promotional share illustration extend the existing storybook direction; they are distinct from gameplay screenshots.

Installed copies receive new levels through the normal site release. Check at launch, foreground return and reconnection. Precache a whole release before offering Update game in a menu, preserve progress and preferences, and never reload an active chase. Another tab accepting an update must not force the current chase to reload. A failed update leaves the previous complete release usable.

## Open Creative Decisions
World names, unicorn names, Glender's base coat, UI/art details and all tuning are provisional. The brief explicitly authorizes independent choices; record them in DESIGN_NOTES.md.
